# Vendor pages on how long visit audio is kept

The report says that the product pages we opened state retention periods for visit audio from no storage to 90 days, and it names the four products. This page lists those pages so that a reader of the repository can check the sentence.

Each page was first opened on Sun 15 Nov 2026 between 13:07 and 13:11 Pacific, and opened again on Sun 15 Nov 2026 by 14:13 Pacific (times from the `date` command). Both times the page was fetched with a plain HTTPS request and read as text. The quotes below were matched against the text of the second opening, and every quote still stands.

| Product page | URL | Date shown on the page | Retention statement for audio, quoted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft, Dragon Copilot frequently asked questions | https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/healthcare/dragon-copilot/about/faqs | "Last updated on 2026-07-27" | "Dragon Copilot retains audio, recognized text, transcript, and flowsheet value data for up to 90 days." |
| Suki, Security FAQ | https://developer.suki.ai/documentation/references/faqs/security | none read | "The original audio recording is permanently deleted after 30 days." |
| Freed, HIPAA compliance in AI scribes | https://www.getfreed.ai/resources/hipaa-compliance-in-ai-scribes | none read | "Audio recordings are not stored and are immediately deleted upon successful note generation, which happens within 60 seconds of the encounter ending." |
| Doximity, Meet Doximity Scribe (blog) | https://blog.doximity.com/articles/meet-doximity-scribe | none read in this pass | "We do not store or retain any audio." |

**Does the report's sentence hold?** Yes, for these four pages. Two pages state that audio is not stored, one states deletion after 30 days, and one states retention for up to 90 days. "Up to 90 days" is a maximum, so the range runs from no storage to at most 90 days.

**Limits.** These are the vendors' own statements, and nothing on the pages lets an outsider check them. Two of the "not stored" statements still describe processing in real time or for up to 60 seconds. The four pages are the ones opened this morning.
